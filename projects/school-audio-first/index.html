School: an audio-first study program

Builder 2026 Python · local text-to-speech · phone web app · spaced-repetition cards
  • Problem: I wanted to keep studying ML, security, markets and software design without sitting at a desk.
  • Built: a lesson pipeline with a fact-check gate. A lesson is not published until each claim in it is checked; the ledger holds 1,654 checked claims so far.
  • Result: 31 lessons across the subject tracks plus a 100-day ML plan, with audio and review cards. It started on Nov 20, 2026, so the result so far is the pipeline, not a streak.

The problem

I lose study time when it depends on sitting down. I wanted lessons I could hear while doing other things, and I did not want to memorise something an AI made up.

What I built

  • A nightly job that drafts short lessons in four subjects and renders them to audio with a local text-to-speech model.
  • A fact-check gate. Every claim in a lesson goes into a ledger and has to be checked before the lesson is published. A lesson edited after its check is held back until it is checked again.
  • A phone page with one next step for the day, a player, and one review queue of cards that come back on a spaced schedule.

Result so far

1,654 checked claims, 31 subject lessons and a 100-day ML plan. The school started on Nov 20, 2026, so there is no long streak to show yet. What exists is the pipeline and the gate, and I use both.
